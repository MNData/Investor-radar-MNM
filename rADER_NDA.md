## 1. Background

The Disclosing Party has developed a proprietary investment intelligence application known as **rADER** (the "App"), which includes novel investment conviction algorithms, radar signal methodology, financial data analysis tools, and related intellectual property. The Disclosing Party wishes to disclose certain confidential information relating to the App to the Receiving Party for the sole purpose of obtaining feedback, discussion, and/or potential personal investment consideration ("Purpose").

---

## 2. Definition of Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party to the Receiving Party, whether disclosed orally, in writing, electronically, visually, or by any other means, including but not limited to:

- The concept, design, and functionality of the rADER application
- Investment conviction algorithms and confidence ratio methodology
- Radar signal generation logic and scoring systems
- Source code, scripts, and technical architecture
- UI/UX designs, wireframes, and prototypes
- Business model, monetisation strategy, and pricing plans
- Financial projections, revenue models, and valuation data
- User acquisition strategies and target market data
- Any third-party data integrations or API methodologies
- Investor pipeline, partnership discussions, or commercial strategies
- Any information marked "Confidential" or that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the disclosure

## 3. Obligations of the Receiving Party

The Receiving Party agrees to:

3.1 **Keep confidential** all Confidential Information and not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent from the Disclosing Party.

3.2 **Use** the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose defined above and for no other purpose whatsoever.

3.3 **Protect** the Confidential Information with at least the same degree of care used to protect their own confidential information, and in any event no less than reasonable care.

3.4 **Not copy, reproduce, or record** any Confidential Information except as strictly necessary for the Purpose.

3.5 **Immediately notify** the Disclosing Party upon becoming aware of any unauthorised disclosure or use of the Confidential Information.

3.6 **Not reverse engineer** any software, algorithm, or technical component shared by the Disclosing Party.

---

## 4. Exclusions from Confidentiality

The obligations in Clause 3 shall not apply to information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:

4.1 Was already in the public domain at the time of disclosure and not through any breach of this Agreement;

4.2 Becomes part of the public domain after disclosure through no act or omission of the Receiving Party;

4.3 Was already known to the Receiving Party at the time of disclosure, as evidenced by written records predating this Agreement;

4.4 Was independently developed by the Receiving Party without reference to or use of the Confidential Information;

4.5 Was lawfully disclosed to the Receiving Party by a third party without restriction on disclosure; or

4.6 Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided that the Receiving Party gives the Disclosing Party prompt prior written notice and cooperates with any reasonable request to seek a protective order.

## 5. Non-Compete Obligation

5.1 The Receiving Party agrees that during the term of this Agreement and for a period of **three (3) years** following its termination or expiry, they shall not, directly or indirectly:

- Develop, build, fund, advise, or participate in any application, platform, or service that is substantially similar to or competitive with the rADER App; or
- Use any Confidential Information to create, contribute to, or improve any competing investment conviction radar, signal intelligence, or portfolio confidence scoring application.

5.2 The Receiving Party acknowledges that the restrictions in this Clause are reasonable in scope and duration given the nature of the Confidential Information disclosed.
